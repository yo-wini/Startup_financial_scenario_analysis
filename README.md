# Startup_financial_scenario_analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a startup’s financial performance under multiple business scenarios (Base, Best, and Worst) to support strategic decision-making. The end-to-end workflow starts with **financial modeling in Excel**, followed by **scenario simulation using Python**, and concludes with a **one-page executive dashboard built in Power BI**.

The project is designed to evaluate profitability, cash runway, customer growth, and cost structure, enabling leadership to make informed funding, pricing, and scaling decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Evaluate startup profitability and break-even timelines
* Analyze cash runway and financial sustainability
* Understand the impact of customer growth on revenue and costs
* Compare financial outcomes across multiple strategic scenarios

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Financial modeling, assumptions, and structure
* **Python (Pandas, NumPy)** – Scenario simulation and automated projections
* **Power BI** – One-page executive dashboard and KPI reporting

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Ending Cash Balance
* Monthly Burn Rate
* Ending Customers
